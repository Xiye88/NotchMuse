Bridging QQ Music to modern macOS

NotchMuse brings clean, native macOS presentation to QQ Music listeners. Instead of keeping a bulky player window or obstructive lyric strip over your workspace, keep synced lyrics tucked cleanly in the menu bar or near the MacBook notch.

Synchronized lyric engine

NotchMuse matches active tracks from public lyric sources and keeps lines aligned with playback progress—supporting word-timed lyrics when the lyric source provides them for smooth, natural reading.

Configuration guide for QQ Music macOS client

  1. Open the QQ Music Mac desktop app and start playing a song.
  2. Launch NotchMuse; it connects to QQ Music via macOS Now Playing playback controls with no manual LRC imports needed.
  3. Pick Status Bar Mode, Notch Mode, or Floating Mode and fine-tune width, opacity, and color presets.

Choosing between Notch, Menu Bar, and Floating display

Use Notch Mode on notched MacBooks to flank the camera housing, Status Bar Mode for a minimal single-line menu bar footprint on any display, or Floating Mode when you want a freely positioned click-through overlay above the Dock or on an external monitor.

Questions before you try it

Does NotchMuse work with the QQ Music Mac app?

Yes. NotchMuse works with the QQ Music Mac desktop app via macOS Now Playing playback controls.

How does NotchMuse impact Mac system performance?

NotchMuse is a lightweight native Swift/AppKit application designed to stay out of your way and run efficiently on Apple silicon.

Can I customize the lyric display layout?

Yes. NotchMuse lets you adjust display widths, color presets, opacity, animation speed, and switch between notch, status bar, and floating modes.

What happens when QQ Music plays a song without lyrics?

NotchMuse neatly falls back to displaying the song title and artist name without breaking layout or producing errors.
