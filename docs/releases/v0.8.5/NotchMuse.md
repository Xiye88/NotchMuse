# NotchMuse v0.8.5 (Build 83)

## English

### Version Display, Smoother Updates & In-App Announcements
- **Clear version display**: Current version and build number are now visible in Settings → General and at the bottom of the Menu Bar dropdown.
- **Clear feedback during background updates**: Automatic launch checks and automatic background downloads remain enabled by default; clicking *Check for Updates…* while a background download is active now displays a clear status notice instead of ignoring the click.
- **Native Updates & Announcements window**: Clicking the pinned top banner opens a native in-app history & announcements window with an unread indicator, quiet 6-hour background refresh, and a direct `support@notchmuse.com` feedback footer.

### Lyrics Engine & UI Highlights (Cumulative from v0.8.3)
- **90% lyrics match rate across 4 players**: Apple Music (43% → 90%), Spotify (58% → 90%), NetEase Cloud (45% → 90%), Soda Music (44% → 90%).
- Direct Kugou & QQ Music lyric sources, Enhanced LRC inline word-timestamp parsing, and frosted Notch / Free Floating UI polish.

## 简体中文

### 版本号展示、自动更新反馈与应用内公告中心
- **清晰展示当前版本号**：设置页「通用」及菜单栏下拉菜单底部均新增当前版本号与 Build 号展示，随时一眼确认。
- **默认开启启动自动检查与自动下载安装**：后台静默下载新版本期间，手动点击「检查更新」不再无响应，改为明确提示当前进度。
- **原生「更新与公告」中心**：点击设置页顶部常驻公告条直接打开应用内窗口查看历史版本与平台公告，支持未读橙色小圆点、每 6 小时静默刷新及底部 `support@notchmuse.com` 直达反馈栏。

### 歌词引擎与界面亮点（累计包含 v0.8.3 全部升级）
- **四平台歌词命中率提升至 90%**：Apple Music（43% → 90%）、Spotify（58% → 90%）、网易云音乐（45% → 90%）、汽水音乐（44% → 90%）。
- 恢复酷狗/QQ 音乐直连歌词源、逐字时间戳解析、跨源时间轴校验以及刘海/悬浮毛玻璃质感升级。
