| --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | 每一句，都在视线里。 | Every line, right in sight. |
| 3 | 一眼歌词，不扰音乐。 | Lyrics at a glance. Music uninterrupted. |
| 4 | 听歌，不必低头找歌词。 | Keep listening. Keep lyrics in sight. |
| 5 | 让歌词自然留在视线里。 | Keep lyrics naturally in sight. |
