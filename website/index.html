Three steps. Then just listen.

  1. Open NotchMuse

    On Mac, open the DMG and drag the app to Applications. On Windows, run the portable .exe. No account is required on either platform.

  2. Play a track

    Start music in one of 39+ compatible players. Allow Automation on Mac if prompted. Windows syncs through the system media session.

  3. Choose a layout

    Use the menu bar or notch on Mac, the taskbar or top island on Windows, or Free Floating lyrics on either desktop.

Frequently asked questions

Which music players work with NotchMuse?

NotchMuse supports 39+ players including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, Soda Music, foobar2000, MusicBee, TIDAL, Qobuz, Roon, Cider, and browser players. See the full compatible players directory.

What Mac do I need?

NotchMuse requires macOS 14 or later and an Apple silicon Mac.

Why does macOS ask for permission?

NotchMuse needs Automation permission to read the selected player’s current track and playback position. The left-side menu bar layout also needs Accessibility permission.

Does NotchMuse upload or listen to my audio?

No. It does not read or upload audio. Track details may be sent to third-party lyrics providers for matching. No NotchMuse account or telemetry is required.

macOS warned me when I opened NotchMuse. What should I do?

Direct downloads are not yet notarized, so macOS may ask you to confirm the first launch. Follow the setup help for the current steps.

How do automatic updates work?

On Mac, open Settings → General → Check for Updates. On Windows, download the latest portable .exe, close the running app, and replace the previous file.

How can I support NotchMuse?

Open Support NotchMuse in the app for WeChat Pay or USDT, or visit the support page.
