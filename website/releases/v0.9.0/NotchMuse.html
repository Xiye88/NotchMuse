NotchMuse v0.9.0

Build 110 · macOS 14+ & Windows 11/10

1. New Vinyl Studio UI & Immersive Lyrics Stage

  • Vinyl Studio workbench: Spinning album-cover vinyl paired with a full-song immersive lyrics stage.
  • Single-click seek: Click any timed lyric line or anywhere on the progress bar once to jump playback immediately.
  • Lyrics Share Card: Generate 3-template shareable lyric posters with album artwork directly from the lyrics stage.
  • Free Floating refinement: Dual-line smooth sweep, 6-key hover bar, 7-color quick palette, compact icon-only 🔒 desktop unlock, and unified Automatic Updates (Recommended) toggle.
