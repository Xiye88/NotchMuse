# NotchMuse v0.9.0 (macOS Build 110 · Windows 0.9.0)

**A milestone dual-platform release — NotchMuse now brings its signature Vinyl Studio UI, Free Floating lyrics, and 800-song precision lyrics engine to both macOS and Windows.**

## Highlights

### 1. Official Windows 11/10 Support (Portable `.exe`)
- **Win11 Bottom-Left Taskbar Lyrics**: Native taskbar integration that keeps synced lyrics cleanly tucked in the bottom-left corner without taking up screen space.
- **Top Floating Island & Free Floating Overlay**: Dual-line smooth sweep, 6-key hover toolbar that auto-flips below near the top edge, 7-color quick palette, center-snap alignment guide, and click-through lock (`🔒`).
- **Zero Install**: Single-file portable `NotchMuse-Windows-win-x64-portable.exe` (74.71 MB), no account and no telemetry.

### 2. New Vinyl Studio Hi-Fi UI & Immersive Lyrics Stage (Mac & Windows)
- **Vinyl Studio Workbench**: Spinning album-cover vinyl record paired with a Soda Music–style full-song immersive lyrics stage.
- **Single-Click Seek**: Click any timed lyric line or anywhere on the progress bar once to jump playback immediately.
- **Lyrics Share Card**: Turn favorite lyric lines and album artwork into clean 3-template shareable posters.
- **Tactile Micro-Interactions**: Spring physics and optional subtle sound cues (`Interactive Micro-Sounds` toggle in Settings).

### 3. Major Lyrics Precision Upgrade (7 Platforms × 800-Song Benchmark)
- **Zero Instrumental False Positives**: Crushed instrumental false positives across all 7 platforms from **32.5% (91/280) → 0.0% (0/280)** by preserving NetEase `pureMusic` evidence, filtering standalone notices (`纯音乐，请欣赏`), and requiring independent corroboration for UGC uploads.
- **Higher Core Studio Accuracy**: Apple Music **96.12%**, QQ Music **96.25%**, NetEase Cloud Music **96.12%**, Spotify **95.25%** across 800 real-world tracks.
- **Smarter Variant & Alias Normalization**: Preserves dashed titles, OST subtitles, and Live markers while rejecting concert albums for studio requests, plus improved Han-script collaboration & artist alias handling.

### 4. Free Floating & Settings Polish
- **Compact `🔒` Desktop Unlock**: Hover locked lyrics to reveal a clean icon-only `🔒` unlock pill directly on the desktop; toggling off position lock in Settings immediately restores free dragging.
- **Simplified Auto-Updates**: Unified into a single **Automatic Updates (Recommended)** switch.
- **39+ Compatible Players**: Works out of the box with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, Soda Music, TIDAL, Qobuz, Plexamp, Roon, MusicBee, foobar2000, and more.
