NotchMuse v0.8.4

Build 82 · macOS 14+

1. Native In-App Announcements & Release History

  • No more forced browser redirects: Clicking the top announcement bar in Settings (or the Menu Bar entry) now opens a clean native Updates & Announcements window right inside NotchMuse to browse announcements and version history.
  • Pinned bar with subtle unread dot: The top announcement bar stays pinned with the latest update instead of disappearing after click, and shows a subtle orange unread dot whenever a new announcement arrives.
  • Quiet background refresh: Automatically refreshes announcements every 6 hours and when reopening Settings, so long-running menu bar sessions stay up to date without restarting.

2. Instant Bilingual Release Notes & Unified Support

  • Zero-JS interactive language switch: Release notes inside the Sparkle update dialog now default to your macOS language and switch instantly between English and Chinese with one click.
  • Unified support channel: All support inquiries now route to support@notchmuse.com, with a direct feedback bar built into the bottom of the Updates & Announcements window.
  • Includes all v0.8.3 upgrades (90% lyrics match rate across Apple Music, Spotify, NetEase Cloud & Soda Music, startup automatic update checks, and Notch/Free Floating UI polish).

3. Feedback & Community

Found a bug or a song missing lyrics? Reach out anytime:

  • Support Email: support@notchmuse.com
  • GitHub Issues: Xiye88/NotchMuse/issues
  • Scan to join the NotchMuse WeChat User Group

1. 原生应用内「更新与公告」中心

  • 不再跳转浏览器：点击设置页顶部公告条（或菜单栏公告入口）将直接打开 NotchMuse 原生「更新与公告」窗口，一站查看平台公告与历史版本更新记录。
  • 公告条常驻 + 未读小圆点：顶部公告条点击后不再消失、固定展示最新一条；当有新公告发布时右侧会出现克制的橙色小圆点，点开查看后自动消除。
  • 定时静默刷新：每 6 小时及重新打开设置页时按需静默刷新公告，菜单栏长期不退出也能及时看到最新消息。

2. 更新说明一键切换与统一反馈邮箱

  • 更新弹窗零延迟中英切换：Sparkle 更新弹窗按系统语言自动展示中文或英文，右上角按钮无需 JS 即可一键切换。
  • 统一反馈邮箱：问题反馈邮箱正式升级为 support@notchmuse.com，并在「更新与公告」窗口底部内置直达入口。
  • 完整包含 v0.8.3 的全部核心升级（四平台歌词命中率提升至 90%、启动自动检查与自动下载安装更新、刘海与悬浮词条质感升级）。
