NotchMuse v0.8.5

Build 83 · macOS 14+

1. Version Display, Smoother Updates & In-App Announcements

  • Clear version display: Current version and build number are now shown in Settings → General and at the bottom of the Menu Bar dropdown.
  • Clear feedback during background updates: Automatic launch update checks and background downloads are enabled by default; clicking Check for Updates… while a background download is running now shows a clear status notice instead of ignoring the click.
  • Native Updates & Announcements window: Clicking the pinned top banner in Settings opens an in-app history & announcements window with a subtle unread dot and quiet background refresh.

2. Major Lyrics Engine Upgrade (90% Match Rate Across 4 Players)

Apple Music43% → 90% (+47%)
Spotify58% → 90% (+32%)
NetEase Cloud45% → 90% (+45%)
Soda Music44% → 90% (+46%)
  • Restored direct Kugou & QQ Music lyric sources, Enhanced LRC word-timestamp parsing, and cross-source timing corroboration across C-Pop, K-Pop, J-Pop, and Western tracks.
  • Upgraded Notch frosted vibrancy with a crisp 0.5pt rim, capsule Free Floating hover bar, and readability shadows.
