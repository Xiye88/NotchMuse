INSTALLATION & SETUP

Ready in a few steps.

NotchMuse v0.8.0 requires macOS 14 or later and an Apple silicon Mac.

  1. Download and open the DMG.
  2. Drag NotchMuse to Applications.
  3. In Applications, Control-click NotchMuse and choose Open.
  4. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  5. Grant the requested permissions when prompted.

Direct downloads are not yet notarized with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS may show a first-launch security prompt.

First-launch steps
  1. Open the DMG and drag NotchMuse to Applications.
  2. Try to open NotchMuse. If macOS blocks it, click Done; do not move it to the Trash.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  4. Authenticate if macOS asks, then open NotchMuse again.

A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Which music players work with NotchMuse?

Spotify, Apple Music, NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, and Soda Music are supported.

What Mac do I need?

NotchMuse requires macOS 14 or later and an Apple silicon Mac.

Why does macOS ask for permission?

NotchMuse needs Automation permission to read the selected player’s current track and playback position. The left-side menu bar layout also needs Accessibility permission.

Does NotchMuse upload or listen to my audio?

No. It does not read or upload audio. Track details may be sent to third-party lyrics providers for matching. No NotchMuse account or telemetry is required.

macOS warned me when I opened NotchMuse. What should I do?

Direct downloads are not yet notarized, so macOS may ask you to confirm the first launch. Follow the setup help for the current steps.

How do automatic updates work?

Starting with v0.8.0, Settings → General → Check for Updates finds and installs signed updates. Manual checks work even when automatic checks are off. If you're using Beta 3 or an earlier version, install v0.8.0 manually once.

How can I support NotchMuse?

Open Support NotchMuse in the app for WeChat Pay or USDT, or visit the support page.
