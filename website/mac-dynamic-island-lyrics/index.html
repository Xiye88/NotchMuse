A Dynamic Island–style music companion for your Mac

NotchMuse places synchronized lyrics cleanly around the MacBook notch cutout for a calm, integrated look at the top of your display. Inspired by Dynamic Island aesthetics, it keeps the current line in view without crowding your workspace.

Want more detail on notch vs. Dock positioning? See our MacBook notch lyrics guide.

Hardware support on Apple silicon

NotchMuse is built as a lightweight native Swift/AppKit app for notched MacBook Pro and MacBook Air models with Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later. It works across Spotify, Apple Music, NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, and Soda Music.

Smooth track transitions and lyrical rhythms

During active playback, NotchMuse keeps lyrics synced with your current song—including word-timed progression when the lyric source provides it—and steps back quietly when playback pauses.

Non-notch and external display flexibility

Plug into an external monitor or use a Mac without a notch? Switch seamlessly to Status Bar Mode or Floating Mode so your lyrics stay readable on any screen setup.

Questions before you try it

What is Dynamic Island–style lyrics in NotchMuse?

NotchMuse creates a sleek visual overlay around the MacBook notch cutout, presenting live synchronized lyrics inspired by Apple’s Dynamic Island design.

Which MacBook models support the notch lyrics layout?

Notched MacBook Pro and MacBook Air models with Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later are natively supported.

What happens if I connect an external monitor without a notch?

When using external monitors or Macs without a camera notch, NotchMuse works cleanly in Status Bar Mode or Floating Mode.

Is NotchMuse an official Apple product?

No. NotchMuse is an independent open-source utility. Dynamic Island and MacBook are trademarks of Apple Inc.
