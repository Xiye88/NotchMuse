# NotchMuse v0.6.0 Post-Release Stability Validation Report

Date: 2026-08-24

## Final Gate

**PASS WITH RISKS**

No crash, stale-lyrics persistence, resource leak, Apple Music data-loss bug,
or Spotify regression was found. One user-visible P1 issue was reproduced in
v0.6.0: Apple Music could briefly show an Automation permission error while a
new track was still becoming readable. The minimal fix is verified in a local
v0.6.1 build 7 candidate but is not published.

## Apple Music Long-run Stability Report

- Exercised the GitHub-downloaded v0.6.0 build 6 for more than 60 minutes.
- Covered 20 normal song changes, 6 rapid changes, English and Chinese tracks,
  pause/resume, previous/next, foreground/background use, no-current-track,
  Music.app restart, and NotchMuse restart.
- The player title and lyric frame changed together; old lyrics were cleared
  during search, player exit, and no-current-track states.
- `Blinding Lights` produced the expected `No lyrics found` state and later
  playback recovered without a restart.
- Music.app exit showed `Apple Music is not running`; reopening Music and
  resuming playback restored current lyrics.
- Re-granting Automation permission restored lyrics without restarting
  NotchMuse.
- No P0 issue was found.

### Confirmed P1

During a search-result transition to `大鱼(唱片版)`, Music.app temporarily had
no readable `current track`. v0.6.0 classified that transient state as
`.unavailable`, displayed an incorrect permission warning, and recovered after
several seconds.

The candidate fix makes the AppleScript return `stopped` when `current track`
is temporarily absent. The same reproduction on v0.6.1 build 7 showed
`Changing song...` at 1 second and current lyrics from 3 seconds onward, with
no permission warning. Matcher, Provider order, retry, score, threshold, and
normalization are unchanged.

## Spotify Regression Stability Report

- Completed 30 minutes of continuous public-build operation.
- Covered 10 consecutive song changes, pause/resume, player exit/restart, and
  operation while Apple Music was also open.
- Rapid changes showed `Changing song...` rather than stale Apple Music or
  Spotify lyrics, then resolved to the final song.
- Spotify exit immediately cleared the lyric and showed `Spotify is not
  running`.
- Spotify restart recovered automatically after the desktop client completed
  its startup registration. The recovery delay is user-visible but did not
  require a NotchMuse restart.
- No functional Spotify regression was found.

## Multi-player Switching Stress Report

- Apple Music and Spotify were open and playing concurrently.
- With Spotify selected, NotchMuse followed Spotify and ignored Apple Music.
- With Apple Music selected, NotchMuse followed Apple Music while Spotify
  remained open.
- Selected-player exit did not fall through to the other player or retain its
  lyrics.
- Post-release stress used persisted Player Source plus controlled app restart;
  the Settings selector path had already passed the v0.6 release gate.
- No duplicated window or persistent mixed-player state was observed.

## Lyrics Display Stability QA Report

- Status Bar long-line frames moved for about 9 seconds, then remained
  byte-identical through second 16.
- Notch Mode long-line frames moved through about second 6, then remained
  byte-identical through second 14.
- A new track reset the animation.
- Short/current lines, Chinese text, English text, and punctuation rendered
  without bounce or continuous autoreverse.
- No persistent flicker or layout growth was observed.

## Failure State Recovery Report

Verified on real apps:

- no lyrics found;
- changing song;
- Apple Music and Spotify with no current track;
- selected player exit and restart;
- Apple Music Automation denial and re-grant;
- recovery from a lyric miss to a later lyric hit.

Provider network disconnection remains unexecuted because changing the Mac
network state requires action-time permission. System sleep/resume also remains
a manual hardware test. These are residual validation gaps, not observed bugs.
